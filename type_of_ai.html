Narrow AI (ANI)

Narrow AI is designed to perform a single task extremely well.

It cannot think beyond its predefined rules or domain.

Examples include spam filters, face recognition, and voice assistants.

×

General AI (AGI)

AGI aims to replicate human-level intelligence.

It can reason, learn, and adapt across many tasks.

AGI is still theoretical and under active research.

×

Super AI (ASI)

Super AI surpasses human intelligence in all fields.

It could outperform humans in creativity and strategy.

ASI raises serious ethical and existential concerns.

×

Reactive Machines

Reactive machines respond only to current inputs.

They have no memory or learning capability.

Classic chess engines are a common example.

×

Limited Memory

Limited memory AI uses recent data to make decisions.

Past experiences improve accuracy and prediction.

Self-driving cars rely heavily on this type.

×

Theory of Mind

This AI attempts to understand emotions and intentions.

It adapts responses based on human behavior.

Still largely experimental.

×

Self-aware AI

Self-aware AI would have consciousness.

It understands its own internal state.

This type does not exist yet.
